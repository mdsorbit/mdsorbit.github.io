Use online calculators with confidence

Calculators are useful for quick estimates, comparisons and everyday conversions. Before relying on a result, check the unit, input range and rounding method used by the calculator.

Simple checking habits

  • Confirm whether a value is entered in metres, centimetres, kilograms, grams or another unit.
  • Check whether the result is rounded and whether more decimal places are needed.
  • For financial estimates, verify fees, taxes, interest frequency and dates with the official provider.
  • For health, legal or safety decisions, do not rely on a general-purpose calculator alone.
  • Repeat important calculations independently when the result affects a payment or decision.

ORBIT's everyday utilities are intended for general information and convenience, not professional financial, medical or legal advice.
