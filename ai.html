How to choose the right AI tool

AI tools are built for different jobs. A chatbot may help draft or explain text, an image generator creates visual concepts, and a transcription service turns speech into text. Start by defining the task you need to complete rather than choosing a tool because it is popular.

Check these points before using one

  • Language support: confirm that the tool handles the language you plan to use.
  • Limits: check daily credits, file-size limits, export quality and whether a free trial expires.
  • Privacy: read what happens to prompts, uploaded files and generated content.
  • Accuracy: verify important facts, calculations and citations independently.
  • Rights: review the provider's rules for commercial use, ownership and permitted content.
