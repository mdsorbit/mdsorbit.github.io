Privacy Policy

This policy explains what visitors should know when using MDS ORBIT, an independent digital discovery website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site currently does not offer user accounts or a first-party account registration system.

Information and hosting

When you visit a website, its hosting and delivery providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested page, and request time for delivery, diagnostics and security. MDS ORBIT is hosted through GitHub Pages. For details about GitHub's processing, read GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Advertising and cookies

MDS ORBIT may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense if the site is approved and ads are enabled. When ads are served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your browser, deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted by your settings and applicable law, personalise advertising. These technologies may process information such as browser identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions and visits to this or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and manage available ad personalisation controls through My Ad Center. The availability of controls may vary by location and account.

Third-party links and services

ORBIT lists links to independent websites, apps and tools. Opening an external service takes you to that provider, which may collect information and use cookies under its own policies. MDS ORBIT does not control those services. Review the provider's privacy information before signing in, uploading files or sharing personal details.

Browser-based tools

Some ORBIT utilities process selected files locally in your browser. The page will describe this where relevant. However, external libraries, fonts, icons or other embedded services may load from their own providers and may receive ordinary connection information. Avoid using browser tools with confidential files unless you understand how the tool works.

Children

MDS ORBIT is a general-audience discovery directory and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external websites and apps.

Policy updates and contact

Visitors in regions where consent is required may be shown a consent choice before personalised advertising technologies are used. Where applicable, visitors can review or change advertising and privacy choices through the consent controls presented on the site. This policy may be updated when site features, advertising or data practices change. The review date above indicates the latest policy edit, not a guarantee that every third-party provider's practices remain unchanged. To ask a question or report a concern, use the Contact page.
