ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Discover your digital universe.

MDS ORBIT is an independent digital discovery platform built to help everyday users find useful websites, apps, AI services, learning resources and simple browser-based utilities without searching across dozens of disconnected lists.

ORBIT is a directory and discovery guide. Most listed services are operated by their own providers; when you choose an external service, you leave ORBIT and use that provider under its own terms and privacy practices.

What you can explore

  • AI tools and assistants
  • Useful apps and learning resources
  • Document, office, image and everyday tools
  • Creative prompts and design inspiration

How resources are selected

Resources are organised by their main purpose and described in plain language. We aim to prioritise tools that have a clear use, a reachable official website and information that can help visitors decide whether to explore them. Listings are not a guarantee of quality, safety, availability or suitability.

Pricing and features can change. A Free, Freemium or Paid label is a quick guide only; check the provider’s current plan details before creating an account or making a purchase.

Independent directory

MDS ORBIT is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the third-party services listed in its directory. Product names and logos belong to their respective owners.

We aim to keep descriptions and links useful, but a listing may become outdated between reviews. If you notice a broken link or incorrect detail, please use the Contact page to report it. Any future sponsored placement or paid promotion will be clearly identified as advertising.
