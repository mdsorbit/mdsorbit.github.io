APP SELECTION GUIDE

Choose an app that fits your device and needs

Before installing an app, identify what you want it to do and whether a web version is enough. An app may offer notifications or offline access, while a browser service can avoid using extra storage.

Review before installing

  • Check the publisher name and official website to avoid lookalike apps.
  • Read recent reviews for recurring complaints, not only the star rating.
  • Review requested permissions and ask whether each one is necessary for the app's main function.
  • Check the last update date, Android or iOS compatibility and minimum device requirements.
  • Look for subscriptions, auto-renewal, in-app purchases and cancellation instructions.
